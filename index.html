A Red, Red Rose 🍂

O my Luve's like a red, red rose,
That's newly sprung in June;
O my Luve's like the melodie,
That's sweetly played in tune.
~ Robert Burns

My Meaning 💌

1. O my Luve's like a red, red rose, 🌹

Poet tar priyo meyetike ekta toktoke lal golaper sathe tulona korchen. Tar chokhe meyeti ekta golaper motoi sundor, komol ar monmugdhokor. Jemon lal golap tar soundorjo diye manusher mon kere ney, temni oi meyetir soundorjo poet-er mon chhuye jay. Ekhane poet sudhu meyetir bairer soundorjo noy, tar proti nijer gobhir bhalobashao prokash korchen....

2. That's newly sprung in June; 🌸

Poet emon ekta golaper kotha bolchen, jeta June mashe notun kore phute utheche. Ei notun phute otha golap tar kache sotejota, komolota ar notun praner protik. Poet-er chokhe meyetio temni sotej, pranobonto ar bishesh—jake dekhle mone ekta mishti anondo jege othe. Meyetir uposthiti jeno tar prithibite notun soundorjo niye esheche....

3. O my Luve's like the melodie, 🎶

Ebar poet tar bhalobashake ekta mishti surer sathe tulona korchen. Jemon ekta sundor sur shunle mon bhalo hoye jay, temni oi meyetir kotha bhable ba tar uposthiti onubhob korle poet-er mon anonde bhore othe. Tar kache meyeti emon ekta mishti onubhuti, ja shudhu chokhe dekhar noy, mon diye onubhob korar bishoy....

4. That's sweetly played in tune. 💗

Poet bolchen, sei surta emon mishti bhabe bajano hoy je shunte ekta alada shanti ar anondo paowa jay. Temni meyetir proti tar bhalobashao tar kache sundor, shantimoy ar mon chhuye jaowa. Tar bhalobashar moddhe jeno ekta nikhut surer mil royeche—jeta tar monke gobhir bhabe chhuye jay. bujhli chokka mal ? 🫩 (sooo cringe 😴) ....
